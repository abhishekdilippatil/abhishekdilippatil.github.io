About

Abhishek is a software-focused digital engineer based in Weimar, Germany, currently completing a Master's in Digital Engineering at Bauhaus-Universität Weimar, with coursework in programming, simulation, and computational methods. His engineering background — three years at AUO Mobility Solutions (formerly Behr-Hella Thermocontrol) building test systems and requirements pipelines for AMG, VW, Stellantis, and TATA — now feeds into software and data-driven work.

Recent projects lean into code: a Python-based autonomous navigation and control system for Boston Dynamics' Spot robot, an interactive data visualization tool for questionnaire analysis, and parametric, script-driven modelling with Dynamo. He speaks five languages and volunteers at a student-run bike repair workshop in Weimar.
